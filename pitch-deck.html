StudyPack Kenya — KCSE & CBC Offline Revision Slide 1 / 6
Slide 1 • Overview

StudyPack Kenya

Offline-first revision platform supporting KCSE (8-4-4) and CBC (Junior & Senior School) learners in low-connectivity and high-data-cost environments.

Repo: https://github.com/Pach3384/studypack-offline-revision.git

Slide 2 • The Problem

Connectivity & Data Cost Barriers

  • Intermittent internet and mobile data costs interrupt revision when students need it most.
  • Heavy web portals fail or time out on low-bandwidth connections.
  • Learners need structured notes, exam/competency tips, flashcards, and practice tests without constant network access.
Slide 3 • The Solution

Lightweight Offline Study Packs

  • Download compact packs (~13–18 KB) covering 12 core KCSE subjects plus CBC strands and revision tips.
  • Study notes, flip flashcards, and take auto-graded quizzes with zero connection.
  • Import past/revision papers (.json, .txt, .pdf) or create custom packs directly in the browser.
Slide 4 • Architecture

Offline PWA + Sync Queue + FastAPI

  • Frontend PWA: index.html, styles.css, app.js, and Service Worker (sw.js) for shell caching.
  • Local Persistence: localStorage caches packs and queues quiz attempts while offline.
  • Backend API: FastAPI (backend/main.py) handles /api/packs, /api/sync, and /api/progress when back online.
Slide 5 • Live Demo Flow

How to Demo in 90 Seconds

  • Switch between All Curricula, KCSE, and CBC and download a pack.
  • Toggle Go Offline, read revision tips and notes, flip flashcards, and submit a quiz.
  • Watch the attempt queue locally, then toggle Go Online (or click Sync Queue) to sync.
Slide 6 • Impact & Next Steps

Built for Inclusive Revision

  • Helps learners revise during outages or low-data periods without losing progress.
  • Gives teachers and peers a simple way to share structured revision bundles and papers.
  • Next steps: expand CBC Senior School strands, larger paper storage via IndexedDB, and persistent database storage.
